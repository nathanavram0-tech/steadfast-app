## Summary
In a 4.5-year randomized trial, adults who used broad-spectrum sunscreen every day showed no detectable increase in skin aging, and about 24% less aging than people who used it when they felt like it.

## Practical application
- Daily broad-spectrum SPF 30+ on face, ears and neck, every morning, including cloudy days. Reapply when outdoors for long.
- It is the best-proven step for skin that looks younger and more even over the years.

## Limitations
Trial in adults under 55 in Australia, with high sun exposure. Measured by skin surface patterns.
