## Summary
Colossians 3:23-24 (World English Bible, verified): "And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ." Paul wrote this to servants in Colossae: ordinary work done wholeheartedly is service to God, whoever the earthly boss or client is.

## Practical application
- Do every client film, edit and email as if for God: on time, honest, and your best quality.
- Your audience is God first, not likes or approval; this cuts people-pleasing and comparison.
- Rest is part of faithful work too; diligence is not burnout.

## Limitations
The verse is about attitude and motive in work; it does not promise financial success. Context: it sits in a household code (Colossians 3:18-4:1) addressed to first-century households, so apply the principle, not the social structure.
