## Summary
Alex Hormozi is an entrepreneur and investor. He opened a gym in 2013, turned his gym turnaround work into Gym Launch (licensed to thousands of gyms) and built Prestige Labs (supplements sold through the same gyms). He sold a majority stake in those businesses in 2021 (reported at $46.2M for 66%). With his wife Leila he runs Acquisition.com, which invests in profitable small companies, and he wrote $100M Offers and $100M Leads.

## Main teachings
- Value Equation: perceived value goes up with the dream outcome and the perceived likelihood of achieving it, and goes down with time delay and effort and sacrifice. To raise value, fix the weakest of the four.
- Grand Slam Offer: stack bonuses, guarantees and scarcity so the offer is clearly worth more than the price, so you compete on value, not price.
- Risk reversal: guarantees lower the buyer's fear.
- Naming: make the offer name speak to a specific person, result and timeframe.
- Fund growth with customer cash flow; favor recurring and referral revenue.

## Failures and mistakes
He went broke twice early on, including after a partnership collapsed. Payment processors held his revenue for months because his business was flagged high-risk. After selling Gym Launch he said he felt poorer because the cash-flow engine was gone. He has publicly broken down his own content mistakes.

## Evidence check
His frameworks are expert opinion from his own industries (gyms, info products), not tested in controlled studies. The value equation lines up with well-known ideas in pricing and consumer psychology, but numbers like "10x value" are rules of thumb.

## Criticisms
Critics say the books simplify things, may not transfer to every industry, pay little attention to ethics and sustainability, and his free content funnels into expensive programs.

## Practical application (videography business)
- Write one clear offer per niche: for example "a 60-second cinematic wedding highlight delivered in 14 days, or the edit is free".
- Raise perceived likelihood with proof: 3-5 sample films, testimonials, a clear process.
- Cut perceived effort: a simple booking link, a shot list template, one call to plan.
- Use referrals and recurring retainers (monthly content for gyms, restaurants, real estate agents) to smooth income.

## Limitations
Sources are secondary profiles and a study guide, not the books themselves; quoted figures (sale price, portfolio revenue) are as reported. Reading his books directly is a remaining gap.
