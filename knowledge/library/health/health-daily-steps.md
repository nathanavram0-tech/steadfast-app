## Summary
More daily steps were linked to lower risk of death, with benefits leveling off around 8,000-10,000 steps a day for adults under 60 and 6,000-8,000 for older adults.

## Practical application
- 8,000-10,000 steps covers most of the health benefit. Higher counts are fine but burn more calories, which must be eaten back on a bulk.
- Walking is a low-fatigue way to stay fit without hurting recovery.

## Limitations
Observational data: association, not proof of cause.
