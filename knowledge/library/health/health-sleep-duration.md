## Summary
Adults should regularly sleep 7 or more hours a night for health. Sleeping less on a regular basis is linked with weight gain, worse mood and performance, impaired immunity and higher risk of several diseases. More than 9 hours may be appropriate for young adults, after sleep debt, or during illness.

## Practical application
- Treat 8 hours in bed as the training target, to get 7+ hours of actual sleep.
- Keep a consistent wake time, even on weekends; it anchors the body clock.
- Morning light, no caffeine within about 8 hours of bed, a cool dark room, and phones out of bed.

## Limitations
Consensus based on reviewing the evidence, mostly observational links rather than trials of sleep extension.
