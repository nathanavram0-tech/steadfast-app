## Summary
Heavy drinking after training reduces muscle building. In a controlled study, a large amount of alcohol after exercise lowered muscle protein synthesis even when protein was eaten with it, though protein reduced the damage.

## Practical application
- Avoid heavy drinking on training days, especially after leg or upper sessions you care about.
- If drinking, keep it moderate, eat protein, hydrate, and do not let it cut sleep.
- Alcohol also adds empty calories and often causes next-day facial puffiness.

## Limitations
One acute study of a large dose in a small group of men; light drinking was not tested.
