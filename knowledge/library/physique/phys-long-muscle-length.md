## Summary
Exercises that load a muscle while it is stretched tend to grow it more than versions that load it shortened. Two well-run trials show this: seated leg curls (hips bent, hamstrings long) beat lying leg curls, and overhead triceps extensions beat pushdowns for triceps growth.

## Key findings
- Maeo 2021: 12 weeks of seated leg curls produced clearly more hamstring growth than lying leg curls.
- Maeo 2023: overhead elbow extensions produced substantially more triceps growth than the same work with arms at the sides, including in the lateral and medial heads.

## Practical application
- Prefer stretch-loaded options: seated over lying leg curls, overhead extensions in addition to pushdowns, incline curls or preacher curls for biceps, deep-stretch flyes, deficit or full-depth calf raises, Romanian deadlifts.
- Use a full range of motion and control the stretched part of the rep instead of bouncing.

## Limitations
Small samples of untrained or lightly trained adults. Not every muscle has been tested, and "lengthened partials" alone are still emerging evidence.
