## Summary
Moderate cardio does not stop you from building muscle, but a lot of it, especially running, can blunt gains in size and strength. The interference grew with more frequent and longer cardio sessions; cycling interfered less than running.

## Practical application
- On a bulk, keep hard cardio to about 2-3 sessions a week of 20-30 minutes; walking (like his step goal) has little interference.
- Separate hard cardio from leg training by several hours, or do it on another day.
- Eat back what cardio burns: 15,000 steps a day can add several hundred calories of expenditure.
- Keep some conditioning for heart health and work capacity; the goal is managing dose, not avoiding cardio.

## Limitations
2012 meta-analysis of mostly short studies; newer studies suggest interference may be smaller in well-fed, well-recovered trainees.
