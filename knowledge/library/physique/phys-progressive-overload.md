## Summary
To keep growing, the work has to get harder over time. Adding weight and adding reps at the same weight both work: in Plotkin 2022, progressing load or progressing reps produced similar muscle growth over 8 weeks, with small differences in strength measures.

## Key findings
- Progressing reps (same weight, more reps) gave similar hypertrophy to progressing load.
- Progressing load was a little better for 1RM squat strength; rep progression was a little better for muscular endurance.

## Practical application
- Use double progression: choose a rep range (for example 8-12). When you hit the top of the range on all sets with good form, add the smallest weight jump and start again at the bottom.
- Log every working set (the app does this) and aim to beat the last session by a rep or a small amount of weight.
- If a lift stalls for 3 sessions: check sleep and food first, then swap the variation or reduce fatigue, before adding more volume.

## Limitations
One 8-week trial in trained-but-not-elite men. Long-term progress also depends on eating enough, sleep and exercise selection.
