## Summary
Short rest (about 1 minute) can reduce how much work you get done in later sets. In trained men, 3-minute rests produced more strength and more growth in some measures than 1-minute rests over 8 weeks.

## Practical application
- Rest about 2-3 minutes on heavy compound lifts, 1-2 minutes on isolation work.
- Rest until you can repeat a quality set, not by a strict clock. If reps crash on set two, rest longer.
- Short on time: use supersets of non-competing muscles (for example biceps with triceps) instead of cutting rest on big lifts.

## Limitations
Single 8-week trial with a small sample. Volume was matched by sets, not by total work, which partly explains the result.
