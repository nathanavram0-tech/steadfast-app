## Summary
Eric Helms is a researcher and coach focused on natural (drug-free) bodybuilding and strength sport. His reviews translate research into practical guidelines for bulking (off-season) and cutting (contest prep).

## Main teachings
- Natural lifters should gain and lose slowly: slow off-season gains limit fat, slow cuts protect muscle.
- High protein during a cut (relative to fat-free mass) helps keep muscle.
- Effort (reps in reserve) and progressive overload matter more than exotic techniques.
- Supplements are minor; creatine and caffeine are among the few with good support.

## Evidence check
His guidance is based on systematic reading of the evidence, but much of the bodybuilding-specific data are case studies and small samples; he flags these limits himself.

## Practical application
For a lean bulk: modest surplus, weekly average weight trend, protein about 1.6-2.2 g/kg, hard progressive training, and patience across months.

## Limitations
Profile based on two of his reviews; his books and later work are not yet researched in this library.
