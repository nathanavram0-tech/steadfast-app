## Summary
Brad Schoenfeld is one of the most published researchers on muscle hypertrophy. His reviews and meta-analyses on volume, frequency, load, rest and protein timing shape much of today's evidence-based bodybuilding advice.

## Main teachings
- Mechanical tension is the main driver of growth.
- Volume has a dose-response relationship with growth; about 10+ hard sets per muscle per week is a good baseline.
- With equal weekly volume, frequency matters little; training each muscle twice a week is a practical default.
- A wide range of loads builds muscle if sets are hard; heavy loads build more strength.
- Longer rests (2-3 minutes) help keep set quality high.

## Evidence check
His positions come from meta-analyses of controlled trials, the strongest evidence available in this field. Their limits: short studies, young subjects, and site-specific muscle measures.

## Where experts disagree
Researchers still debate how high volume should go before returns flatten, and how much stretch-position training adds.

## Practical application
Use his findings as the default rules for programming, then adjust to personal recovery, schedule and lagging muscles.
