## Summary
Strength training is one of the best-supported ways to prevent sports injuries. In a meta-analysis of randomized trials, strength training cut sports injuries to under a third, while static stretching showed no protective effect.

## Practical application
- Warm up with lighter sets of the first lift instead of long static stretching.
- Progress loads gradually (small jumps, logged), keep 1-2 reps in reserve on heavy compound lifts, and use controlled technique.
- Train the back and rear delts at least as much as pressing to balance the shoulders.
- Sharp or worsening joint pain is a stop signal: swap the exercise and see a physio if it persists.

## Limitations
The trials were in sports populations, not specifically bodybuilders, and injury definitions varied.
