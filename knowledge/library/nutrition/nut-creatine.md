## Summary
Creatine monohydrate is the most researched sports supplement. The ISSN position stand concludes it improves high-intensity exercise capacity and lean mass gains from training, and is safe for healthy people at the intakes studied (commonly 3-5 g per day, sometimes after a short loading phase).

## Practical application
- Monohydrate is the form with the evidence; other forms are not shown to be better.
- Daily consistency matters more than timing.
- Expect 1-2 lb of early weight gain, mostly water inside muscle; that is not fat.
- Anyone with kidney disease or on medications should check with a doctor first.

## Limitations
Position stands are written by experts in the field. Claims of hair loss come from one small study measuring DHT, not hair loss itself; it has not been replicated.
