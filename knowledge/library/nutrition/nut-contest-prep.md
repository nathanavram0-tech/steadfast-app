## Summary
When cutting, slow fat loss and high protein protect muscle. The review recommends losing about 0.5-1% of body weight per week, protein of about 2.3-3.1 g per kg of fat-free mass, fat at about 15-30% of calories, and carbs filling the rest to support training.

## Practical application
- Cut only when the bulk has added enough fat to justify it; at under 10% body fat, a lean bulk is usually the better use of time.
- Never use dehydration, diuretics or extreme deficits. They cost muscle and can be dangerous.
- Keep lifting heavy and hard during a cut; it is the signal to keep muscle.

## Limitations
Much bodybuilding-specific data are case studies; recommendations are a synthesis, not tested as a full protocol.
