## Summary
Eating more protein helps muscle gain from lifting up to about 1.6 g per kg of body weight per day on average, with the upper confidence limit near 2.2 g/kg. Above that, there was no further measurable benefit for most people.

## Key findings
- Protein supplementation increased gains in fat-free mass and strength from training.
- The benefit plateaued around 1.62 g/kg/day (95% CI up to about 2.2 g/kg/day).
- The effect is larger in trained lifters than beginners, and smaller with age.

## Practical application
- At 79 kg (175 lb): about 127 g/day covers most people; 175 g/day covers the high end. A 200 g target is safe for a healthy person but not required; extra calories may be better spent on carbs for training.
- Total daily protein matters most; food or shakes both count.

## Limitations
Most included trials were 6-24 weeks. Needs during a cut (calorie deficit) are higher; see the contest-prep review.
