## Summary
The body uses protein from large meals too; the old "30 g per meal" limit is a myth for total use, though muscle building per meal may plateau. A practical target is about 0.4 g/kg per meal across at least four meals (up to about 0.55 g/kg per meal for the high end of daily needs).

## Practical application
- Spread protein over 4-5 meals of roughly 30-45 g for a 79 kg lifter, including breakfast and a meal within a few hours around training.
- Exact post-workout timing matters little if daily protein is high and meals are spread out.
- A pre-bed protein meal (for example Greek yogurt) is a reasonable way to add one more feeding.

## Limitations
Recommendations are extrapolated from acute muscle protein synthesis studies, not long-term growth trials.
