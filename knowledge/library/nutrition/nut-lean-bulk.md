## Summary
A trained lifter can only build muscle so fast, so a big surplus mostly adds fat. Reviews suggest a modest surplus and a slow gain of about 0.25-0.5% of body weight per week. In elite athletes, a guided surplus added more lean mass than eating freely, but also more fat.

## Practical application
- At about 175 lb: aim for roughly 0.4-0.9 lb per week on the 7-day average.
- Adjust calories by about 100-200 kcal only after 2-3 weeks of trend data.
- Track waist weekly: if the waist climbs fast while strength stalls, the surplus is too big.
- Keep protein about 1.6-2.2 g/kg and fill the rest with carbs to fuel training.

## Limitations
The off-season review is narrative and the trial was in elite athletes over 8-12 weeks. Individual gain rates vary widely.
