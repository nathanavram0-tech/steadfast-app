## Summary
Caffeine reliably improves many types of performance, including strength and endurance. The ISSN reports benefits at about 3-6 mg per kg taken about an hour before, with lower amounts also helping some people. The cost is sleep: in a controlled study, a large dose (400 mg) even 6 hours before bed cut sleep by more than an hour.

## Practical application
- Use the smallest amount that works and keep it at least 8 hours before bed for evening sleepers.
- Count all sources: coffee, pre-workout, energy drinks.
- If training in the evening, consider caffeine-free sessions to protect sleep, which matters more for growth.

## Limitations
Response varies with genetics and habitual intake. People with heart conditions, anxiety or high blood pressure should check with a doctor.
