## Summary
In negotiation, the first number on the table tends to pull the final deal toward it. Galinsky and Mussweiler found that whoever made the first offer usually got the better outcome, and that the anchor's pull could be weakened when the other side focused on the first mover's alternatives or on their own target.

## Practical application
- Quote first when you know the market: name a confident, well-justified package price (for example a premium wedding package), then explain the value.
- When a client anchors low, do not argue the number. Shift focus to the outcome and your package options, and restate your own target.
- Prepare your walk-away price and your best alternative before any call.

## Limitations
Lab negotiations with students; real deals involve relationships and repeat business.
