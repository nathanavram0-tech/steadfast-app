## Summary
Turning a goal into a specific "if situation X, then I will do Y" plan makes people much more likely to follow through. A meta-analysis of 94 studies (over 8,000 people) found a medium-to-large effect (d = 0.65) on goal attainment.

## Practical application
- Write if-then plans for the moments you usually fail: "If it's 5:30 pm on a weekday, then I drive straight to the gym in my gym clothes."
- Plan for obstacles: "If I'm tempted to skip the meal prep Sunday, then I start with just cooking the rice."
- Make the "when" and "where" exact. Vague intentions are what fail.

## Limitations
Effects are smaller for very complex goals and when the underlying goal is weak.
