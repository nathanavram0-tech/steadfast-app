## Summary
The popular idea that willpower is a fuel tank that runs out with use is disputed. A preregistered replication across 23 labs and over 2,000 participants found an effect close to zero. Self-control is better built by designing your environment and routines than by trying to "save" willpower.

## Practical application
- Remove decisions: lay out gym clothes, prep meals, keep junk food out of the house, phone outside the bedroom.
- Make the good choice the easy default and the bad choice inconvenient.
- Do not tell yourself you "used up" willpower; that belief itself may make you quit sooner.

## Limitations
The debate continues; some researchers argue the replication task was too weak. Treat "willpower tank" claims as unsupported, not as proven false.
