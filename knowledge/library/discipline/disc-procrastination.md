## Summary
Procrastination is mostly about motivation over time, not laziness. Steel's meta-analysis found the strongest predictors were how unpleasant the task feels, how far away the reward is, low confidence you can do it, and impulsiveness and distractibility.

## Practical application
- Shrink the task until it is not aversive: "edit the first 30 seconds of the wedding film", not "edit the film".
- Bring the reward closer: set a near deadline, tell the client a date, reward yourself right after.
- Build confidence with quick wins first.
- Remove distractions during work blocks (phone in another room, one tab).

## Limitations
Mostly correlational data from students and self-reports.
