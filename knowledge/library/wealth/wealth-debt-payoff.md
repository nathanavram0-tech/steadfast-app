## Summary
Math says pay the highest interest rate first (avalanche). Behavior research says small wins keep people going: in data from about 6,000 debt-settlement clients, closing more individual accounts predicted finishing the program, even for the same dollars repaid. Lab work found people finish tasks faster when the parts go from smallest to largest.

## Practical application
- If rates are close, or motivation is the problem, pay minimums on all and attack the smallest balance first.
- If one debt has a much higher rate (for example a credit card at 25%+), attack that first.
- Never miss minimum payments; set autopay.

## Limitations
The debt data came from people in a settlement program; dollar repayment and accounts closed were highly correlated. General education only.
