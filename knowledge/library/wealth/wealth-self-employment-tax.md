## Summary
Profit from a side business like videography is subject to self-employment tax of 15.3% (12.4% Social Security plus 2.9% Medicare) on top of income tax. You must file Schedule SE when net self-employment earnings are $400 or more. The employer-equivalent part of SE tax is deductible when figuring adjusted gross income. Self-employed people may need to pay quarterly estimated taxes.

## Practical application
- Set aside a share of every client payment (many self-employed people use about 25-30%) in a separate tax savings account until you know your real rate.
- Track business expenses (camera gear, software, mileage) to reduce taxable profit.
- Check whether quarterly estimated payments apply (IRS Publication 505) once side income becomes regular.
- A tax professional is worth it the first year the business is meaningful.

## Limitations
General education, not tax advice. Thresholds and rules change; re-verify each year.
