## Summary
Even small amounts of easy-to-reach savings make a big difference in avoiding debt spirals. When the CFPB launched its savings initiative, it noted that 40% of Americans could not cover a $400 emergency from their own savings. A starter cushion comes before investing aggressively.

## Practical application
- Step 1: a starter fund of about one month of essentials in a separate high-yield savings account.
- Step 2: build toward 3-6 months of essentials; aim for the higher end with irregular self-employment income (videography).
- Automate a transfer on payday so saving happens before spending.
- Use the fund only for true emergencies and refill it first afterward.

## Limitations
This is general education, not personal financial advice. The 3-6 month range is a common planning convention, not a law.
